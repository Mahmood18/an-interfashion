      Who we are

      Built on experience.
      Focused on your order.

      A.N. Inter Fashions is a Karachi-based textile manufacturer specializing in knitwear, fleece apparel, terry towels, institutional towels, barmops, and a wide range of customized textile products for international export markets.

      Our facility is equipped with modern machinery, supported by a team of skilled professionals and qualified administrative staff. With strong in-house manufacturing capabilities and an efficient production setup, we are well-equipped to handle bulk orders and meet the diverse demands of international export markets.

      We serve buyers across North America, Europe, and the Middle East, offering a diverse range of products, including T-shirts, fleece sweatshirts, hoodies, joggers, pajamas, polo shirts, terry towels, institutional towels, and barmops. Our production is tailored to meet individual customer specifications and market requirements, ensuring consistency in fabric, fit, quality, and finishing.

      Facility

      Our production facility combines skilled craftsmanship with efficient manufacturing processes to ensure consistent quality. Equipped with 100+ modern machines, we have the capacity to handle bulk orders while maintaining production efficiency. Every new order begins with guidance from our experienced stitching masters, ensuring our workforce understands each customer's requirements.

      From cutting to stitching, every stage follows customer-approved samples and specifications, using techniques that minimise fabric wastage.

      Quality is maintained through inspections at different production stages, with our dedicated QC team monitoring every step. Once approved, finished products are carefully packed by our experienced packing team, ready for shipment.
