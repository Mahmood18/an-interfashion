About Us

A.N. Inter Fashions is a Karachi-based garment manufacturer specializing in basic knitwear and fleece apparel for international export markets, with a working production capacity of approximately 9,000 dozen per month.

Founded in 1996 by Sheikh Irshad Ahmed, the company operates its own cutting, stitching, and finishing facilities from a 15000 sq. ft. production unit at the industrial hub of Karachi. Our team of 150+ skilled professionals manages the complete production process under one roof, including cutting, sewing, buttonholing, eyeleting, pressing, and finishing.

We serve buyers across North America, Europe, and the Middle East, supplying a wide range of essential apparel including T-shirts, fleece sweatshirts, hoodies, joggers, pajamas, and polo shirts. Our production is tailored to meet each market's specific fabric, fit, quality, and finishing requirements, enabling us to deliver consistent products that meet international buyer expectations.
